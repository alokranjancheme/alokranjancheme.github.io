Research Mission

I became a chemical engineer to solve real problems. Early in my career, I realized that chemistry happens at the molecular scale—understanding it requires computational methods and careful molecular-level thinking.

My experience in hydrogen production at ONGC showed me that industrial processes can be dramatically improved through better molecular understanding and computational design. This motivated my focus on sustainable computational chemical engineering.

Today, I pursue research at the intersection of molecular dynamics simulations, machine learning, and chemical engineering principles. I want to develop the next generation of computational tools for understanding and designing sustainable chemical processes.

My long-term vision is to become a leader in computational chemical engineering, contributing both to fundamental science and to solving real-world sustainability challenges—particularly those relevant to developing countries like India.
