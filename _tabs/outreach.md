## Scientific Communication & Community Engagement

I believe scientific knowledge should be accessible to everyone. As part of my commitment to science outreach, I created and maintain [**tendstozero.com**](https://tendstozero.com).

---

## tendstozero: Making Chemistry Accessible

[tendstozero.com](https://tendstozero.com) is a free educational platform dedicated to making chemistry and scientific knowledge accessible to students and the general public.

### Mission

Democratize access to high-quality scientific education. Too often, advanced chemistry concepts are hidden behind textbooks and paywalls. tendstozero makes them free and interactive.

### Content

The platform covers:
- **General Chemistry**: Fundamentals of chemistry, atomic structure, bonding, thermodynamics
- **Computational Chemistry**: Introduction to molecular simulations and computational methods
- **Chemical Engineering**: Process fundamentals, reactor design, unit operations
- **Sustainability**: Green chemistry, renewable energy, circular economy
- **Career Development**: Guidance for chemistry students and aspiring researchers

### Audience

- Chemistry students (secondary and undergraduate)
- Career changers interested in science
- Science enthusiasts
- Young people exploring STEM

### Impact

- Free access, no ads, no paywalls
- Written for clarity and understanding
- Connects theory to real-world applications
- Encourages critical thinking about science

## Why Outreach Matters

### Personal Motivation

When I was studying chemistry, I struggled to find resources that explained "why" beyond just formulas. I want to create the resources I wished existed.

I come from India and benefited from free educational resources online. Now I want to give back by creating free resources for students everywhere.

### Scientific Responsibility

As researchers, we have a responsibility to communicate our work clearly to the public. Science should not be confined to academic journals read only by other scientists.

## My Philosophy on Science Communication

1. **Clarity over Complexity**: Explain ideas simply without losing accuracy
2. **Context Matters**: Connect concepts to real-world applications and motivations
3. **Accessibility First**: Make knowledge free and available to all
4. **Critical Thinking**: Encourage questions and deeper understanding
5. **Long-term Impact**: Build resources that last and evolve
